Send photos to a Frameo frame or a group of frames from Homey Pro. Choose a JPEG or PNG on your phone, or automate sends with a URL or image tag in a Flow. Browse photos in the gallery, choose one to display on the frame, or add a photo widget to your dashboard.

This independent community app requires an ARM64 Homey Pro with Homey software 12.9.0 or later. It was tested on a Homey Pro (2023) with a Pexar frame. Pair with the frame’s friend code. For the gallery, widgets and media counts, allow the Homey sender to view photos in Settings → My friends on the frame. Displaying, hiding or deleting a photo also requires permission to manage photos.
